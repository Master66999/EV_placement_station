01 • GOVT SUBSIDY & IT DEPRECIATION SAVE ₹3.5L – ₹5.0L

PM E-DRIVE Capital Subsidy & 100% Depreciation

Claim state EV policy capital subsidies on Fast DC equipment plus 100% Accelerated Depreciation under Income Tax Section 32 in Year 1, effectively cutting net hardware investment by 22–30%.

02 • ROOFTOP SOLAR PV INTEGRATION SAVE 28–35% OPEX

30kW Canopy Solar Net-Metering

Co-locate a 30kW rooftop solar array over charging bay canopies. Solar generation reduces daytime grid power consumption from ₹6.80/kWh down to ~₹2.10/kWh net-metered cost.

03 • DYNAMIC LOAD MANAGEMENT (DLM) AVOID ₹4.0L GRID COST

Smart Peak Shaving & Load Sharing
